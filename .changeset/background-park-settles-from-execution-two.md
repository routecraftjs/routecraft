A background tool over a route that parks settles from execution two, and the tool's shape follows the route's shape (#813).

- **The handle stays open through the park.** A `directTool` dispatch that resolves with a `Deferred` acknowledgment no longer settles the handle as failed with `AI1006`: the call stays in `background` on the session record with the park's `deferralId` recorded beside the handle, and the model keeps holding a `running` handle. The handle and the session ride the dispatched exchange's headers (`AgentHeadersKeys.BACKGROUND_HANDLE`, the new `AgentHeadersKeys.BACKGROUND_SESSION`), survive the park with it, and the agent runtime settles the handle from execution two's terminal event: the continuation's body on `route:exchange:completed`, a failure carrying the error code on `:failed` or the drop reason on `:dropped`, and `RC5047` on `route:exchange:expired`, mapped through the recorded `deferralId`. The mapping lives on the session record, so a restart between the park and the decision loses nothing: a parked call is not reported lost at boot. `AI1006` remains for a route that answers with another route's acknowledgment without parking itself.
- **The tool's shape follows the route's shape.** A direct tool over a route that can park (`routeCanDefer`) is background without being declared so, in `functions: { ask: directTool(id) }` and `tools(["Direct(id)"])` alike; `background: false` on such a route fails `context.start()` with `RC5003` naming the route. A route that does not park behaves exactly as before. `tools([...])` accepts `{ name, background }` on a `Direct(<routeId>)` reference, and the tool keeps its `direct__<routeId>` name.
- **`agentPlugin({ reidentify })`** re-verifies, from live state, the identity a parked session exchange carried before its continuation revives on a background settlement. The framework refuses the revival unless the answer is live-branded and the same identity as the parked principal, compared on the set `.resume({ elevate })` compares with `scopes` added on the subject and on every actor; with no hook, a revival over a principal-bearing exchange is refused rather than run restored, the settlement is still written to the inbox, and `route:agent:session:revival:refused` records the reason. An anonymously dispatched agent revives as before.
- **Breaking** (`@routecraft/ai`, internal surface): `AgentSessionRuntime.settleBackground(key, outcome)` reads the tool, the caller and the start time from the record, so `BackgroundOutcome` carries only the handle and the outcome; the in-memory `backgroundOrigins` map is gone, the origin is persisted on `AgentBackgroundCall.origin`.
- **Core**: `Execution.resume(request, options?)` takes `RevivalOptions` with `reidentified`, the parked identity re-verified live, applied once the claim is won and refused with `RC5056` when restored or different; `PluginRoutes.canDefer(id)` answers whether an exchange on a route can park; `reidentificationDeviation` and `deferredPrincipal` are exported for a plugin that drives a revival under a re-verified identity.
